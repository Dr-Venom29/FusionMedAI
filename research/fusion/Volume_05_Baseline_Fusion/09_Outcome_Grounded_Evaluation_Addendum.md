## 1. Context & Scientific Question

While the primary Volume 05 analysis established that **ACARA-U (B6)** redistributes decision weights dynamically across available channels based on confidence, reliability, uncertainty, and quality ($H(w) = 1.0176$ vs Uniform $H(w) = 1.0986$), those historical evaluations operated over an uncalibrated controlled decision cohort where ground truth patient outcomes were unlinked.

This addendum answers the outcome-grounded question:

## 3. Key Findings & Honest Decision Rule Verdict

1. **Statistically Significant Modest Improvement (Practical Threshold Not Met)**:
   Across 10 independent confirmatory cohorts ($5,000$ packets), full ACARA-U (B6) achieved a statistically significant reduction in predictive error compared to B5 ($\Delta_{\mathrm{MAE}} = -0.001843$, $95\%$ Hierarchical CI: $[-0.002112, -0.001582]$, cohort $t = -20.30, p = 7.95 \times 10^{-9}$).
   - **Honest Reporting Statement**: While the error difference consistently favors B6 across all cohorts, the effect size did **not** meet the prespecified practical superiority threshold ($\Delta_{\mathrm{MAE}} < -0.005$).

2. **Simulated Quality-Term Contribution (H2 & H3 Confirmed Observed Benefit)**:
   The observed predictive-error reduction of B6 over B5 in this simulated ablation is directly mediated by the quality term ($\eta=0.5$) under signal degradation scenarios:
   - **Accurate Quality Detection ($N=3,775$):** $\Delta_{\mathrm{MAE}} = -0.002544$, $95\%$ Subgroup CI: $[-0.002820, -0.002268]$ (Error significantly reduced in simulation).
   - **Severe Degradation ($N=385$):** $\Delta_{\mathrm{MAE}} = -0.015416$ (approximately $17.16\%$ relative MAE reduction over B5; conditional on the synthetic degradation operators and quality-sensor detection assumptions).
   - **Undetected Degradation ($N=406$):** $\Delta_{\mathrm{MAE}} = -0.000417$ (Defaults to near B5 parity).
   - **False Alarm Quality Drop ($N=390$):** $\Delta_{\mathrm{MAE}} = +0.001481$ (Slight error increase from attenuating an uncorrupted channel).
   - **Uncertainty Miscalibration ($N=429$):** $\Delta_{\mathrm{MAE}} = -0.000043$ (Robust to imperfect uncertainty proxies).

3. **Singleton Modality Invariance (H4 Confirmed)**:
   In single-modality regimes (`R`, `F`, `C`), $w_i \equiv 1.000000$ for both B5 and B6, resulting in $\Delta_{\mathrm{MAE}} = 0.000000$. The predictive differences between B5 and B6 occur exclusively in multi-modality combinations (`FR`, `CR`, `CF`, `CFR`).

## 4. Methodological Scope & Artifact Integrity

- **In Silico Oracle Basis:** These results establish that B6 improves estimation accuracy under a controlled synthetic data-generating process with defined noise, error, and degradation characteristics. They represent an observed ablation difference in simulation, not demonstrated clinical causation.
- **Sensor Dependence:** The advantage of B6 relies upon quality sensors that accurately identify corrupted modality inputs. Under conditions where degradation is undetected (`MISLEADING_UNNOTICED`), B6 performance defaults to B5 parity.
- **Artifact Verification Checks:** All reported findings were verified against the locked JSON artifacts in `experiments/fusion/outcome_evaluation/` using independent cryptographic SHA-256 hash checks and recomputation routines in `verification/fusion/outcome_evaluation/verify_outcome_artifacts.py` (8/8 gates passed: OE-01 through OE-08).
- **Clinical Non-Efficacy Statement:** These findings demonstrate mathematical and software simulation properties; they do not constitute empirical evidence of patient-level clinical efficacy.
