## 3. Cryptographic Manifest Verification Chain
The evaluation pipeline implements a four-level hierarchical integrity chain:
1. **Level 1 (Upstream Inputs)**: `upstream_packet_manifest.json` guarantees bitwise provenance of the 500 standardized multimodal decision packets generated under deterministic seed 115.
2. **Level 2 (Execution Artifacts)**: Deterministic evaluation runs produce artifacts `02_evaluation_config.json` through `08_execution_summary.json`, capturing tabular results, canonical records, transitions, bootstrap distributions, and runtime diagnostics.
3. **Level 3 (Runner Manifest)**: `09_artifact_manifest.json` cryptographically seals all Level 1 and Level 2 output files via SHA-256 hashes and byte lengths.
4. **Level 4 (Master Freeze Manifest)**: `freeze_manifest.json` is generated by `verify_decision_evaluation_artifacts.py`. It verifies the Level 3 manifest, evaluates all 16 verification gates, and seals both the 11 data artifacts and the 14 research documentation chapters into an immutable audit trail.
